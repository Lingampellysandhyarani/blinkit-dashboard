# Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

I developed a **Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard using Power BI** to analyze grocery sales data and generate meaningful business insights.

The main objective of this project was to understand **sales performance, product performance, outlet performance, sales trends, and customer ratings** through an interactive dashboard.

## Data Preparation & Analysis

I started by cleaning and transforming the data using **Power Query**.

I worked with **six tables** and created relationships between them to connect sales, product, and outlet information.

I then created DAX measures such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Items
- Average Rating
- Average Sales per Outlet

Finally, I used these measures to create **KPI cards, charts, filters, and interactive slicers** for the dashboard.

## Dashboard & Key Insights

The dashboard provides a quick overview of Blinkit's business performance.

- **Total Sales:** Approximately 1.20 million
- **Total Items:** Approximately 9K
- **Average Rating:** 3.92 out of 5
- **Average Sales per Outlet:** Approximately 75.11K

### Key Findings

- **Fruits and Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** are among the top-performing categories.
- **Tier 3 outlets** have the highest sales among the outlet location categories.
- **Medium-sized outlets** show strong sales performance.
- **Regular products** generate higher sales compared with Low Fat products in this dataset.
- The sales trend shows a noticeable increase around **2018**.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I identified a few areas for improvement:

- Maintain sufficient inventory for top-performing categories to meet customer demand.
- Promote low-performing products through discounts and marketing campaigns.
- Continuously monitor outlet performance to identify areas for improvement.
- Monitor customer ratings to improve customer satisfaction.

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how **Power BI can transform sales data into meaningful business insights**.

The dashboard provides a clear view of business performance and can support **better data-driven decision-making**.
